Vision pipeline

Hakimo.ai

The gateway, ingestion layer and edge inference behind Scan, Hakimo's real-time vision pipeline.

Problem
Keep real-time vision in step with 2M+ security events a day, across 5,000+ cameras and 150+ customers.
Approach
Co-architected the gateway and ingestion layer. Profiled edge inference on YOLOv11 and RF-DETR, and kept that path in-house.
Result
~60% less CPU at the edge, and ~$500K of projected infrastructure spend avoided.

Operator console

Hakimo.ai

A live monitoring console where security operators watch camera streams and act on events.

Problem
Operators need hundreds of camera streams live in one view, with no lag between what happens and what they see.
Approach
A WebSocket monitoring console with an NVR-style grid that renders streams in parallel.
Result
Hundreds of parallel camera streams at sub-second latency.
