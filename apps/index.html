Prentice

Launching soon
What
A workshop for the agents you train, running on your own Mac. An agent is a folder you own: a prompt, the direction you've folded in, and a manifest.
How
Prentice schedules it, sandboxes it, records what each run did and cost, and gives you a Desk to judge its work. Six screens: Desk · Review · Agents · History · Spend · Policy.
